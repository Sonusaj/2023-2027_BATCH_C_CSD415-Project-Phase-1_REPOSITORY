Five Stages of Diabetic Retinopathy

Retina-Scan classifies retinal fundus images into five diabetic retinopathy severity categories.

01

No DR

No visible signs of diabetic retinopathy.

02

Mild DR

Early retinal changes such as microaneurysms.

03

Moderate DR

Increased retinal abnormalities and vascular changes.

04

Severe DR

Extensive retinal abnormalities with higher risk.

05

Proliferative DR

Advanced stage associated with abnormal new vessels.
