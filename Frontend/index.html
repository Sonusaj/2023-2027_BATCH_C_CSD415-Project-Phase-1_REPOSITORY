ICDR REFERENCE STANDARDS

Five Stages of Diabetic Retinopathy

Progressive vascular changes and characteristic lesion manifestations defined across retinal fundus photography.

Stage 0

No DR

No visible vascular abnormalities, microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates present in the fundus.

Stage 1

Mild DR

Characterized strictly by the presence of isolated, early-stage microaneurysms within retinal capillaries.

Stage 2

Moderate DR

Microaneurysms along with hard exudates, cotton-wool spots, or moderate intraretinal hemorrhages.

Stage 3

Severe DR

Extensive intraretinal hemorrhages in multiple quadrants and definite venous beading without neovascularization.

Stage 4

Proliferative DR

Advanced stage marked by neovascularization of the disc/retina or preretinal / vitreous hemorrhages.

Architectural Specifications

EfficientNetB0 + Multi-Scale Spatial Attention (MSAG)

Developed in TensorFlow and Keras, our diagnostic system integrates transfer learning with EfficientNetB0 and an MSAG module inserted after the final convolutional block. This configuration computes multi-scale spatial weights to emphasize subtle early lesions while maintaining a lightweight footprint of ~5.3 million parameters.
